# Code of Conduct

OpenApps is a collaborative project. Participants must be respectful, assume good
intent, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized behavior, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions,
or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private contact
options. Do not include sensitive personal information in public issues.
